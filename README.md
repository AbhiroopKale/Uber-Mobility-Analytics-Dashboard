# 🚗 Uber Mobility Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze Uber ride-booking data and uncover insights across bookings, revenue, vehicle performance, customers, payment methods, distance, and cancellations.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project was developed as a **hands-on learning project while learning Power BI through a guided YouTube tutorial**.
@The-Developer-BI 

The tutorial was used as a reference to understand the process of building a professional dashboard, including **data preparation, DAX calculations, KPI creation, visualization selection, and interactive report design**.

The objective was not simply to replicate the tutorial, but to understand the concepts and analytical logic involved in transforming raw ride data into a business-oriented dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze Uber ride-booking performance
* Track revenue and completed bookings
* Compare performance across vehicle types
* Analyze monthly booking and revenue trends
* Understand customer behavior
* Analyze payment methods
* Examine cancelled and incomplete bookings
* Track distance and rating metrics
* Practice creating interactive Power BI dashboards

---

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### 🏠 Overview

The overview page provides a high-level summary of the dataset through:

* Completed Bookings
* Lost Bookings
* Revenue
* Total Distance
* Average Distance
* Monthly Booking Trends
* Revenue by Vehicle Type
* Top Pickup Location
* Top Drop Location
* Average Driver Rating
* Average Customer Rating

### 🚘 Vehicle Analysis

The vehicle analysis page compares:

* Customer Count
* Revenue
* Completed Bookings
* Contribution %
* Monthly Booking Trends

Vehicle categories include:

* Auto
* Bike
* Go Mini
* Go Sedan
* Premier Sedan
* Uber XL

### 💰 Revenue Analysis

The revenue section focuses on:

* Monthly revenue trends
* Revenue by vehicle type
* Revenue by payment method
* Highest revenue month
* Customer-level revenue contribution

### 👥 Customer Analysis

The customer section explores:

* Customer count
* Monthly customer trends
* Customer revenue
* Completed bookings
* Lost bookings
* Average distance
* Payment method usage
* Cancellation-related information

---

## 🔎 Business Questions

The dashboard can help answer questions such as:

* Which vehicle type generates the highest revenue?
* Which month recorded the highest revenue?
* Which payment method contributes the most booking value?
* Which vehicle categories have the highest booking volume?
* Which customers generate the most revenue?
* How significant are lost or cancelled bookings?
* How does customer activity change over time?
* How do revenue and booking volumes vary across vehicle types?

---

## 💡 Key Observations

Some observations from the dashboard include:

* **Auto** is one of the strongest revenue-generating vehicle categories.
* **March** recorded the highest monthly revenue in the analysis.
* **UPI** represents the largest payment method by booking value.
* Different vehicle categories show noticeable differences in booking volume and revenue contribution.
* Lost and cancelled bookings represent an important area for further operational analysis.

> **Note:** These observations are based on the dataset used for this learning project and should not be considered current Uber business statistics.
